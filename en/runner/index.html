iCode Runner

The Mac companion app: run the app you write in iCode in your Mac’s real iOS Simulator, and drive it from your iPad or iPhone.

Download iCode Runner for Mac

Version 1.1 · 3.9 MB · macOS 14 Sonoma or later · signed and notarized by Apple

What you need

A Mac running macOS 14 Sonoma or later, with Xcode and its iOS simulators installed. The Mac and the iPad (or iPhone) must be on the same Wi-Fi network. The Mac simulator is part of iCode Pro; the built-in SwiftUI preview stays free.

Install

  1. Download iCode-Runner.dmg on your Mac. From the iPad, tap “Send link to Mac” in iCode (AirDrop or email).
  2. Open the file, then drag iCode Runner to the Applications folder.
  3. Launch it from Applications: its icon appears in the menu bar, at the top right of the screen. The app is signed and notarized by Apple.

Pair your iPad or iPhone

  1. Click the iCode Runner icon in the menu bar, then “Pair an iPhone or iPad”. A 6-digit code appears.
  2. In iCode: Settings › Mac companion, choose your Mac and enter the code.
  3. In the preview, pick a simulator in the destination bar and tap Run.

See and touch the simulator from the iPad

To show the simulator’s screen on your iPad and drive it with your finger, macOS asks for two permissions the first time. In System Settings › Privacy & Security, turn on iCode Runner in Screen Recording and Accessibility, then relaunch it.

Open your Mac’s terminal

From iCode Runner 1.1, iCode opens your shell (zsh) on the Mac, in the project folder: the Terminal button in the top bar on iPad, the … menu › Terminal on iPhone, the bottom panel (⌃`) on Mac. Commands run on your Mac, never on the iPad. To turn it off, uncheck “Allow the terminal from iCode” in the iCode Runner menu. The terminal is part of iCode Pro.

Uninstall

Quit iCode Runner from the menu bar, then move it to the Trash from Applications.
