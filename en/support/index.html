Support

A question, a bug or an idea? We’ll get back to you.

Contact us

Email kevinm@kmcapitalgroup.fr. For a bug, include your macOS version, your iCode version (iCode menu › About) and the steps to reproduce it.

Install iCode

Download iCode from the download page, open iCode.dmg and drag iCode to the Applications folder. iCode is free, signed and notarized by Apple: macOS opens it without a warning.

Add an AI key

Settings › AI Assistant › pick your provider, then paste your key. The app links to the page where each provider issues keys. Without a key, the editor, the terminal and Git are fully functional.

Connect to a private repository

With GitHub, use “Sign in with GitHub” in the Git panel: no token to create. For another host, open “Remote and token…” and paste a personal access token; it is stored in the Keychain.
