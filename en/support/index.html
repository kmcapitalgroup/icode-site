Support

A question, a bug, an idea, or want to join the beta?

Contact us

Email kevinm@kmcapitalgroup.fr. For a bug, include your device, iOS version and the steps to reproduce it.

Join the TestFlight beta

Send us an email with your Apple ID address and the subject “iCode beta”. You’ll get a TestFlight invitation. During the beta you can also reply straight from TestFlight with “Send Beta Feedback”.

Add an AI key

Settings › AI Assistant › pick your provider, then paste your key. The app links to the page where each provider issues keys. Without a key, the editor and Git are fully functional.

Connect to a private repository

In the Git panel, open “Remote and token…” and paste a personal access token from your host (GitHub, GitLab…). It is stored in the Keychain.

Keyboard shortcuts

With a keyboard attached to your iPad, hold ⌘ to see every available shortcut.

Manage or cancel your subscription

iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your projects stay on your device.
