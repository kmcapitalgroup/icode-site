Privacy policy

iCode is a code editor and Git client that runs on your device. Here is what is processed, where, and what never is. Last updated: November 22, 2026.

No data collection

iCode has no account and no telemetry, ads or tracking. KM Capital Group never receives your code, your files or usage statistics.

Your projects

Your projects, settings and Git repositories are stored in iCode’s container on your device. Widgets read a summary (project name, branch, latest commits) shared locally between the app and its widgets.

API keys and Git tokens

Your AI provider keys and Git access tokens are stored in your device’s Keychain. They are only ever sent to the service they belong to.

Apple’s on-device AI

The free assistant uses the Apple Intelligence model running on your device: your request and your code never leave it.

Cloud AI (optional, with your key)

If you connect a provider (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Mistral, OpenRouter or your own server), iCode asks for your permission first. Then your request and the project files the assistant reads go straight from your device to that provider, with your key, and are handled under its privacy policy. iCode keeps no copy anywhere but on your device. You can withdraw permission in the app’s Settings.

Voice dictation

If you dictate a request to the assistant, iCode uses the microphone and Apple’s speech recognition, on the device when it can, otherwise through Apple’s speech recognition service. Audio is neither recorded nor sent to KM Capital Group.

Remote Git and databases

Clone, pull, push and pull requests connect directly to the Git server you set up (GitHub, GitLab or any other). Databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL, MariaDB) connect directly to the server you set up; their passwords stay in the Keychain. Nothing goes through our servers.

iCloud, sharing and SharePlay

If you share a project, it is stored in your iCloud (CloudKit) and visible only to the people you invite. A SharePlay session exchanges your edits live with participants, through Apple. KM Capital Group has no access to this data.

iCode Runner on your Mac

To run an app in the Simulator, iCode sends your project to iCode Runner on your Mac over your local network, after pairing with a code. Nothing goes over the internet or through our servers.

Crash reports

iOS hands iCode crash and hang reports (MetricKit) that stay on the device. They only leave it if you choose to share them from Settings.

Purchases

On iPhone and iPad, purchases are handled by Apple’s App Store: iCode never receives or stores payment data. On the Mac app downloaded from this site, payment is handled by Stripe; our licence server keeps the licence key, the purchase email and the ID and name of activated Macs, to check the licence and resend a lost key.

Your rights

Almost everything stays on your device: you can remove a key in Settings at any time, and erase everything by deleting iCode. For a Mac licence, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data through the support page.
