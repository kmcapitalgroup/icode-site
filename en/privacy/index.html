Privacy policy

iCode is a code editor and Git client that runs on your device. Here is what is processed, where, and what never is. Last updated: November 12, 2026.

No data collection

iCode has no account, no server and no telemetry. KM Capital Group never receives your code, your files or usage statistics.

Your projects

Your projects, settings and Git repositories are stored in iCode’s container on your device. Widgets read a summary (project name, branch, latest commits) shared locally between the app and its widgets.

API keys and Git tokens

Your AI provider keys and Git access tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain. They are only ever sent to the service they belong to.

AI assistant (optional)

The assistant only works once you add a key. When you use it, your request and the file excerpts it needs go straight from your device to the provider you picked (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Mistral, OpenRouter or your own server). That data is then handled under that provider’s policy. iCode keeps no copy anywhere but on your device.

Remote Git

Clone, pull and push connect directly to the Git server you set up (GitHub, GitLab or any other). Nothing goes through our servers.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple’s App Store. iCode never receives or stores payment data.

Your rights

Everything stays on your device. You can remove a key from the app’s Settings at any time, and erase everything by deleting iCode.

Controller

KM Capital Group, publisher of iCode. Questions: support page.
